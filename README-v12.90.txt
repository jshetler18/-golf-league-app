v12.90 changes
- Replaced the previous low buzzer with a softer two-pulse descending error chime plus vibration when supported.
- Added a second OCR pass focused specifically on the lower scorecard settings area for better recognition of Stimp, Gimmies, Elevation, Wind, Fairways, Greens, Mulligans, and Pins.
- Improved elevation OCR matching for common reads such as 2,000 / 2000 / 2 000 and OCR substitutions such as O for zero, I/l for one, and Z for two.
- Photo Library input is restricted to supported image MIME types only. The app exposes only Take Photo and Photo Library buttons. Note: iOS/Android may still show an OS-owned “Choose File/Browse” entry in their native picker; web apps cannot suppress that system option.
