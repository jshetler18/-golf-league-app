v13.21 - Head-to-head Recorded Rounds score layout

- Matchup score panels now appear underneath the matchup name, month/year, and round information.
- The left score panel follows the first team named in the video matchup description.
- The right score panel follows the second team named in the video matchup description.
- Example: Team Shetler vs Team Shingler shows Team Shetler on the left and Team Shingler on the right.
- Each current/future matchup panel shows Raw Score, monthly Handicap, and Adjusted Score.
- Past-season scoring behavior remains unchanged.
- Single-team recorded round cards remain unchanged.
- No database changes.
