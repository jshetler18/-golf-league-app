v13.44 - Automatic monthly course tee mapping
- Adds Course Tee Boxes & Yardages under Team Handicaps in Monthly Setup.
- Admin enters the actual course tee color/name and yardage for Forward, Senior, Middle, Back, and Tips.
- Reads each player's Official Tee Box from Players & Teams and automatically maps that player to the corresponding course tee and yardage.
- Saving Monthly Setup writes the automatically matched course tee/color and yardage into the player's monthly tee assignment.
- Player Tee Assignments are grouped by team instead of one long player list.
- Includes migration 20260907_add_monthly_course_tee_boxes.sql; migration was also applied to the connected live Supabase project.
