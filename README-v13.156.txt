v13.156 — Simulator late booking + partial cancellation

- Today's hourly slots remain bookable until 15 minutes before the slot ends.
  Example: 8:00–9:00 AM remains available through 8:44 AM and becomes Time Passed at 8:45 AM.
- Time Passed slots retain the light gray/dimmed inactive appearance.
- Multi-hour reservations can now be partially cancelled by individual hour.
- Cancellation popup includes Cancel All plus each hourly segment.
- Remaining reserved hours stay booked and cancelled hours immediately reopen.
- Admin cancellation push notification reports only the hour(s) the player cancelled, in one notification.
