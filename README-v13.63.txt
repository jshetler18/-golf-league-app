v13.63 - Monthly course tee colors and yardages

Rules & Settings > Monthly Settings > Tee Boxes & Yardages:
- Now displays the actual course tee color/name configured by the admin for the selected month.
- Yardage comes directly from that month's Course Tee Boxes & Yardages admin setup.
- The tee color square also matches the actual configured course tee color.
- This changes automatically from month to month because each course can use different tee colors and yardages.

Player Tee Box Assignments:
- Remains unchanged from v13.61/v13.62 and continues to show the league level: Forward, Senior, Middle, Back, or Tips.
