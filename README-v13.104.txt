v13.104 - Month-specific handicap publishing workflow

- Adds a dedicated Save Draft button for monthly team handicaps.
- Adds Use All Available Recommendations.
- Adds month-specific Confirm & Publish [Month] Handicaps button.
- Published months clearly show Published; unpublished months show Draft.
- Publishing first saves every current handicap selection, then creates/updates the published snapshot used by Teams & Handicaps > Handicaps.
- Player page stays frozen until Publish is used.
- Republishing a published month is supported and re-notifies players.
- No database migration required.
