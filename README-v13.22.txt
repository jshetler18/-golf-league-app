v13.22 - Official custom matchup thumbnails

- Recorded Rounds matchup videos now use a custom league thumbnail instead of the YouTube thumbnail.
- The thumbnail includes matchup teams, month/year, round, season, play graphic, and score information.
- Team order follows the video matchup description from left to right.
- Past matchup rounds display RAW SCORE only because historical monthly handicaps are not stored.
- 2026-2027 and future matchup rounds display RAW SCORE, HANDICAP, and ADJUSTED SCORE.
- Matchup information is no longer repeated underneath the thumbnail.
- Tapping the custom thumbnail still opens/plays the YouTube recording in the same card.
- Single-team recordings are unchanged in this version.
- No database changes.
