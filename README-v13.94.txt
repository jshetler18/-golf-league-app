v13.94 — Open Meeting Roster Auto-Sync

- Open meeting RSVP links now add any active player who was added to that season after the meeting was created.
- The same existing RSVP link can be sent to the newly-added player.
- New player appears under their active team and can choose I'm Coming or Can't Come.
- Admin RSVP totals also synchronize when the admin opens the meeting dashboard.
- Closed meetings do not synchronize, preserving historical attendance.
- Existing RSVP responses are never overwritten by roster synchronization.
