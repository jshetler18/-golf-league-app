v13.72 - Teams & Handicaps

Home
- Renamed Teams to Teams & Handicaps.
- Moved History to the bottom of the main home menu, with Rules & Settings directly above it.

Teams & Handicaps
- Teams tab is the default and preserves the existing Teams page content.
- Added Handicaps tab modeled after Rules & Settings tabs.
- Handicap rankings sort active teams highest-to-lowest by calculated raw scoring average.
- Shows Raw Average and current calculated Help / Hurt handicap using the active season Handicap Standard.
- Positive values add points; negative values subtract points.
- Tapping a team expands its handicap calculation detail, showing the recent rounds, Counted/Not Counted status, calculation method, and Handicap Raw Scoring Average.
- Teams without enough scoring history display N/A and sort to the bottom.

No database changes.
