Rules & Settings > Monthly Settings

Tee Boxes & Yardages
- The square uses the ACTUAL course tee color configured by the admin for that month.

  Forward Tees
  Senior Tees

Player Tee Box Assignments

- The square beside each player now uses the exact ACTUAL course tee color assigned to that level for the selected month.
- Therefore the player square matches the same color shown in the Tee Boxes & Yardages key above.
