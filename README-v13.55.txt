v13.55 - Hide unused tee boxes in Monthly Settings

Rules & Settings > Monthly Settings > Tee Boxes & Yardages:
- A tee level is shown only when at least one player is assigned to that course tee for the selected month.
- If no players are assigned to a tee level, that tee level is hidden from this section.
- Tee levels continue to display as Forward Tees, Senior Tees, Middle Tees, Back Tees, and Tips Tees.
- Player Tee Box Assignments remains unchanged.
- No database changes.
